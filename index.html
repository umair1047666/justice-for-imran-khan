The case, in brief

On 16 November 2026, Imran Khan wrote an open letter to Pakistan's senior judiciary from his cell. This is what it says — and what the UN has already found.

  • UN verdict already given: In Opinion No. 22/2024, the UN Working Group on Arbitrary Detention found Mr Khan's detention arbitrary, without legal basis and apparently politically motivated, and called for his immediate release and compensation. Pakistan has not complied.
  • Who he is: Prime Minister of Pakistan 2018–2022, founder of the PTI, captain of the 1992 World Cup-winning cricket team, and founder of the Shaukat Khanum Cancer Hospital.
  • How he got here: Removed by a no-confidence vote in April 2022; arrested on 5 August 2023 and in prison since. In the February 2024 elections, candidates backed by him won the most seats as independents after his party lost its electoral symbol.
  • Solitary confinement: He states he is kept alone for up to 22 hours a day, since 20 December 2025.
  • Vision failing: He states he has lost 85% of the vision in his right eye.
  • Blood pressure 192/193: Recorded on 10 November 2026, according to the letter.
  • Court order ignored: The Supreme Court of Pakistan's order of 18 August 2026, for his medical care and rights, has never been implemented.
  • His wife too: According to the same letter, Bushra Bibi suffered a detached retina in April 2026.
